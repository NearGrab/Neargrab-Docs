# 💡 Product Overview

**Neargrab** bridges the invisible gap between local shopkeepers and nearby customers through a location-based digital marketplace built on social connectivity.

While fast-delivery and mega e-commerce platforms continue to dominate online shopping, they come at the cost of the slow fading of trusted local community shopkeepers. Neargrab aims to give local sellers a modern digital presence, ensuring customers get what they need instantly and locally.

> *"Neargrab doesn’t replace local stores, it revives them."*

---

## Key Challenges Solved

### For Local Businesses
- Struggling to reach digital-first buyers nearby
- Unable to compete with the visibility of giant online marketplaces

### For Customers
- Leaving stores disappointed because they do not know prior to visiting whether a nearby shop holds a particular product in stock

---

## The Solution Ecosystem

A low-friction platform providing local shopkeepers with simple, digital storefronts that can be updated in real-time with zero technical skills (and native language support).

Customers can comfortably search a designated kilometer radius, assess product availability, compare local prices, view reviews, and navigate to local shops immediately.

---

# 🎯 Target Audience / User Persona

NearGrab is designed for three primary user groups:

1. Customers  
2. Local Shopkeepers  
3. Platform Administrators

---

# 1. Customers

## Overview

Customers are individuals who prefer shopping from nearby physical stores but often struggle to find which local shop has the product they need.

They prefer offline shopping because it offers:

- Instant product availability
- Better product inspection
- No delivery waiting time
- More trust in local sellers

However, customers face problems such as:

- Difficulty finding nearby product availability
- Lack of product and stock information
- Uncertainty about shop location and pricing

---

## How NearGrab Helps

NearGrab helps customers:

- Search products available nearby
- Discover local shops easily
- View product availability, ratings, and reviews
- Compare nearby options
- Navigate directly to shops using Google Maps
- Chat with shopkeepers for inquiries

Users can explore products without login, while advanced features like navigation, reviews, and chat require authentication.

---

# 2. Local Shopkeepers

## Overview

Local shopkeepers and small business owners mainly depend on walk-in customers and word-of-mouth marketing.

Many struggle because:

- Their shops lack online visibility
- Nearby customers cannot discover their products
- Competing with large e-commerce platforms is difficult

---

## How NearGrab Helps

NearGrab provides shopkeepers with a simple digital storefront where they can:

- Create and manage shop profiles
- Add, edit, and update products
- Upload product images/videos
- Manage stock availability
- Reach nearby customers digitally
- Communicate with customers through chat
- Promote products without technical expertise

The platform is designed to be easy-to-use and supports local language accessibility.

---

# 3. Platform Administrators (Admin)

## Overview

Admins are responsible for managing and maintaining the platform ecosystem.

They ensure the platform remains safe, organized, and trustworthy for both customers and shopkeepers.

---

## How NearGrab Helps Admins

NearGrab provides admin tools to:

- Manage users and shopkeepers
- Monitor products and remove spam listings
- Verify accounts
- Manage sponsored banners
- Pin featured products on the homepage
- Track platform activity through dashboards

This helps maintain platform quality and ensures a smooth user experience for all users.

# 🚀 Core MVP Features

Here are the core Minimum Viable Product (MVP) features.

---

# A. Authentication & User System

## 1. Secure Authentication

Users can sign up and log in using:

- Phone OTP
- Email & Password
- Google Login

This ensures secure and easy access for both customers and shopkeepers.

---

## 2. Guest Browsing

Users can explore products and shops without creating an account initially.

This reduces friction and improves user engagement.

---

## 3. User Profile System

Each user gets a personalized profile containing:

- Name
- Profile image
- Saved products
- Reviews
- Shop details (if seller)

---

## 4. Become Shopkeeper Feature

Any normal customer account can later become a shopkeeper account by setting up a shop profile.

This removes unnecessary role-selection friction during onboarding.

---

# B. Product Discovery Features

## 5. Nearby Product Search

Users can search for products available in nearby local shops.

---

## 6. Smart Filters & Sorting

Users can filter products based on various characteristics.

---

## 7. Personalized Home Feed

The home feed displays:

- Trending products
- Nearby shops
- Sponsored banners
- Pinned products
- Recommended products

This helps users discover products easily.

---

## 8. Product Detail Page

Every product has a dedicated page.

---

## 9. Nearby Shop Discovery

Users can discover local shops nearby and view:

- Shop ratings
- Products
- Location
- Contact details

---

# C. Shopkeeper Features

## 10. Shop Profile Management

Shopkeepers can create and manage their digital storefront.

---

## 11. Product Listing & Catalog

Shopkeepers can:

- Add products
- Edit products
- Delete products
- Upload product images/videos
- Update stock availability

All products appear inside their catalog page.

---

## 12. Shopkeeper Dashboard

This helps shopkeepers manage their business digitally.

---

## 13. Product Availability Management

Shopkeepers can mark products as:

- In Stock
- Out of Stock

---

# D. Community & Interaction Features

## 14. Product Reviews & Ratings

Customers can:

- Rate products
- Write reviews
- Upload photos

---

## 15. Chat / Inquiry System

Customers can directly communicate with shopkeepers.

---

## 16. QR Code Sharing

Each shopkeeper profile includes a unique QR code available in the dashboard.

---

# E. Location & Navigation Features

## 17. Google Maps Integration

Users can open shop locations directly in Google Maps for easy navigation.

---

## 18. Distance-Based Results

Products and shops are displayed according to the user’s nearby radius/location.

---

# F. Admin Features

## 19. Admin Dashboard

Admins can monitor:

- Total users
- Total shops
- Total products
- Platform activity

---

## 20. User Management

Admins can:

- View users
- Suspend accounts
- Remove fake accounts
- Verify shopkeepers

---

## 21. Product Management

Admins can:

- Review products
- Delete spam/inappropriate listings
- Manage reported products

---

## 22. Sponsored Banner Management

Admins can:

- Add sponsored banners
- Edit banners
- Delete banners
- Schedule promotional content

These banners appear on the home feed.

---

## 23. Pin Product Feature

Admins can pin selected products on the homepage to increase visibility.
